Short Biography

David Davis was born in Moscow, studied at a music college, which he graduated with honors, then studied at the Moscow State Conservatory named after P.I. Tchaikovsky, majoring in choir conductor and at the same time studying composition.

Even from the first grade of music school, David began to compose, and the very first short plays were often performed by other students of the music school. While studying in college, he was a laureate of several composing competitions. While studying at the conservatory, his works were performed on the stages of concert halls.

Now the composer writes, creates and travels around the world, and is always open to creativity and suggestions
